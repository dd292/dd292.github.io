Daksh Dhingra

PhD. Robotics and Controls | Sim2Real Researcher

Austin, TX

I am a system identification and controls engineer at Apptronik, where I narrow the sim-to-real gap for the Apollo humanoid. I earned my PhD from the University of Washington, advised by Dr. Sawyer Fuller. My thesis produced dynamic models and robust controllers for insect-scale flapping-wing robots—expertise I now apply to humanoids. I received my undergrad in Mechanical Engineering from the J.C. Bose University, Haryana, India.

My interests include system identification, reinforcement learning, and robot manipulation. Before my Ph.D., I completed my M.S. at the University of Washington, exploring topics such as deep reinforcement learning, computer vision, probabilistic robotics, and motion planning.

During my PhD, I interned at several research labs. In Winter 2022 and Spring 2023, I was a Research Intern at the Honda Research Institute in Mountain View, CA, working with Nawid Jamali’s team. In the summer of 2018, I was a Deep Learning Intern at Volvo Cars.

Off the robot floor, you’ll find me hiking with my dog Korra, climbing mountains, or carving fresh snow.
