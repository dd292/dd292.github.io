I am a system identification and controls engineer at Apptronik, where I narrow the sim-to-real gap for the Apollo humanoid. I earned my Ph.D. from the University of Washington, advised by [Dr. Sawyer Fuller](https://faculty.washington.edu/minster/). My thesis produced dynamic models and robust controllers for insect-scale flapping-wing robots—expertise I now apply to humanoids. I recieved my undergrad from the J.C. Bose University, Haryana, India.

My interests include system identification, reinforcement learning, and robot manipulation. Before my Ph.D., I completed my M.S. at the University of Washington, exploring topics such as deep reinforcement learning, computer vision, probabilistic robotics, and motion planning.

During my Ph.D., I interned at several research labs. In Winter 2022 and Spring 2023, I was a Research Intern at Honda Research Institute in Mountain View, CA, working with Nawid Jamali’s team.

Off the robot floor, you’ll find me hiking with my dog Korra, climbing mountains, or carving fresh snow
